When Is Changing Jobs for Money Worth It?

A higher salary can improve your financial situation, but it may come with increased stress, longer hours, or reduced work-life balance. The best decision depends on your priorities and long-term goals.

If your current job is causing stress or burnout, consider evaluating your situation carefully before making a move.
