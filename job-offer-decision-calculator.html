How to Use the Job Offer Decision Calculator

A job offer is about more than the number on the paycheck. Salary, benefits and financial security matter, but so do career growth, work-life balance, company culture and the way the job fits into your life.

Rate each factor from 1 to 10 based on the offer you are considering. There is no objectively correct score. A factor that is extremely important to you may deserve a much higher personal rating than it would for someone else.

The final score gives you a quick overview of how attractive the opportunity looks based on your ratings. More importantly, look at the individual scores. A very low score in an important area may deserve further investigation before you accept the offer.

What Matters Most in a Job Offer?

Salary is one of the most obvious factors when comparing jobs, but accepting a new position can affect many areas of your life. A higher-paying role may involve a longer commute, greater stress, fewer flexible hours or weaker career prospects.

On the other hand, a job offering slightly less money could provide better work-life balance, stronger career development or a healthier workplace. The best choice depends on what you value most at this point in your life.

If the offer looks attractive financially but you are already feeling overwhelmed by your current job, it may also be worth looking at your current situation before making a decision.

Frequently Asked Questions About Job Offers

How do I compare multiple job offers?

Compare salary and total compensation, career growth, work-life balance, job security, company culture, flexibility and your personal priorities. Using the same factors for each offer can make the differences easier to see.

Should I accept a job offer with a higher salary?

A higher salary can be an important reason to change jobs, but it should be considered alongside workload, stress, career growth, flexibility, job security and work-life balance.

What should I consider before accepting a job offer?

Consider salary and benefits, career growth, work-life balance, job security, company culture, management, flexibility, commute and how well the position fits your personal goals.

Should I negotiate a job offer?

Negotiating can be worthwhile when there is room to improve salary or other employment terms. Depending on the employer, you may be able to discuss compensation, benefits, flexibility, vacation or other conditions before accepting.

Can a lower salary job be a better choice?

Yes. A lower salary can still be worthwhile if the position offers better career growth, work-life balance, flexibility, security or long-term opportunities.
