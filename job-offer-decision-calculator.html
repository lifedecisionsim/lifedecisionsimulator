Job Offer Decision Calculator

Receiving a job offer can be exciting, but deciding whether to accept it is often difficult. Many professionals focus only on the salary increase, but a smart decision requires looking at multiple factors.

A higher salary might seem attractive, but what about the commute, work-life balance, stress level, or career growth opportunities? These elements can have a big impact on your long-term happiness and financial future.

Why Job Decisions Are Difficult

Job changes are major life decisions. Accepting a new role may mean relocating, adapting to a new team, or taking on more responsibility. Even when the salary is higher, the overall impact on your life is not always obvious.

That is why many people struggle to compare two job opportunities objectively.
