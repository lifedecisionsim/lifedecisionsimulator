When Should You Take a Break From Work?

If you feel constantly tired, unmotivated, or emotionally drained, your body may be signaling that you need rest. Taking a break at the right time can prevent long-term burnout and improve both performance and well-being.

However, if your stress is persistent even after rest, it may be a sign that deeper changes are needed.
