Understanding Burnout

Burnout can affect your health, productivity, and career satisfaction. Our Burnout Risk Calculator helps you measure your risk and take preventive action.

Why Check Your Burnout Risk?

  • Identify early signs of stress overload
  • Adjust work hours and responsibilities
  • Improve mental and physical well-being
  • Maintain long-term career performance

Tips to Reduce Burnout

  • Take regular short breaks
  • Prioritize tasks and set boundaries
  • Practice mindfulness or light exercise
  • Seek professional support if needed
