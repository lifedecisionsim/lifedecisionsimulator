Signs of Burnout and How to Avoid It

Burnout is a state of chronic stress that can severely impact your productivity and well-being. Recognizing the signs early allows you to take action and prevent long-term effects. Our Burnout Risk Calculator can help you evaluate your current stress levels.

Common Signs of Burnout

  • Exhaustion – feeling constantly tired and drained
  • Detachment – losing interest or feeling disconnected from work or responsibilities
  • Reduced performance – struggling to concentrate or complete tasks

How to Avoid Burnout

  • Set clear boundaries between work and personal life
  • Take regular breaks and prioritize rest
  • Practice mindfulness, meditation, or relaxation techniques
  • Seek support from colleagues, friends, or professionals

By recognizing burnout early and using tools like the Burnout Risk Calculator, you can take steps to maintain your mental health and long-term productivity.
